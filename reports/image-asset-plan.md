# Peaks2Shores — image asset plan

What every image change in this pass used, and what still needs an owner's decision.

**Rules followed**

- No hotlinking: every asset is served from `public/`.
- No watermarked previews.
- Nothing bought or downloaded from stock libraries.
- Images go through `next/image` (WebP, quality 85).
- Credits flow from `public/images/credits.json` to `CREDITS.md` via `node scripts/source-photography.mjs --credits`.

**Status key**

- **Live:** in use in this build.
- **Owner action:** needs a decision, a licence record or a photograph from the client.
- **Recommended:** a suggestion that hasn't been applied.
